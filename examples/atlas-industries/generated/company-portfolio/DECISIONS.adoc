[[ADR-PORT-001]]
== ADR-PORT-001: Compose products through published contracts

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-09-19

=== Context

Portfolio governance must not depend on product internals.

=== Decision

Allocate company requirements only to product contract capabilities.

=== Consequences

- Products and shared modules can version independently behind stable contracts.
