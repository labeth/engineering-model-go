[[ADR-COMP-001]]
== ADR-COMP-001: Model the company baseline as a reusable module

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-09-19

=== Context

Every product must demonstrate the same foundational controls.

=== Decision

Publish control implementations, verification, evidence, risk, and POA&M together.

=== Consequences

- Products allocate assurance requirements to one stable contract.

[[ADR-COMP-002]]
== ADR-COMP-002: Centralize employee lifecycle controls

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-09-19

=== Context

Personnel screening, awareness training, role changes, and offboarding are company responsibilities shared by every product.

=== Decision

Model people-security controls once in the shared compliance module and inherit them through the company control-baseline capability.

=== Consequences

- Products reuse consistent organizational controls and evidence without copying human-resources processes.
