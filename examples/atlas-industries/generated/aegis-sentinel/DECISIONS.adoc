[[ADR-AEG-001]]
== ADR-AEG-001: Keep sensor fusion product-specific

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-09-19

=== Context

Aegis sensing algorithms and hardware define product differentiation.

=== Decision

Reuse company platforms while retaining sensor fusion and mission coordination locally.

=== Consequences

- Shared controls evolve independently from mission algorithms.

[[DEC-TIER-MVP]]
== DEC-TIER-MVP: aegis sentinel runs at the mvp tier with the foundation bundle

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-11-12

=== Context

The example is a complete model at one stated tier so that the required lifecycle switches (ADR-EMG-061) are met and its generated artifacts show the diagnostics of that tier.

=== Decision

Tier: MVP
Bundles: foundation
The example declares lifecycle tier mvp with requirementLinks strict and lintRun mode strict.

=== Consequences

- The strict requirement link rules apply from the mvp tier.
- The tier rules of mvp key the severity of the example's findings.
