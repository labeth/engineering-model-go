[[ADR-ORI-001]]
== ADR-ORI-001: Keep adaptive radio scheduling product-specific

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-09-19

=== Context

Orion radio behavior and constrained-link scheduling define product differentiation.

=== Decision

Reuse company platforms while retaining link scheduling and routing locally.

=== Consequences

- Shared controls evolve independently from radio algorithms.
