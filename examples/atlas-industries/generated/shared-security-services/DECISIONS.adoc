[[ADR-SEC-001]]
== ADR-SEC-001: Centralize identity policy but federate enforcement

Status:: accepted
Date:: 2026-09-19

=== Context

Products need consistent identity semantics without a shared failure domain.

=== Decision

Centralize credential policy and require local enforcement at product boundaries.

=== Consequences

- Products consume one identity contract and retain local authorization.
